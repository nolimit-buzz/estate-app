Magodo Residents Association Executive Functional Blueprint

Estate Operating Platform & Governance System

An exhaustive architectural breakdown of the single-estate, multi-tiered software ecosystem engineered for Hierarchical Zonal Multi-Tenancy, Sub-5s Gate Access, Omnichannel Automated Messaging, Zero-Leakage Finance, and Rapid Emergency Dispatch.

Core Architectural Tenet: One estate, one ground truth. Paper gatebooks, fragmented WhatsApp group notices, and handwritten bank payment slips are eradicated. Every physical entry, levy assessment, maintenance work order, and security incident is cryptographically linked to a verified unit and resident dossier.

Multi-Tier Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Portal / Tier Administrative Capabilities Data Boundary & Privacy Scoping
Central Super Admin
MRA Apex Executive		 Global estate settings, payment gateway keys, zonal boundary mapping, guard shift rosters, ID card generator, bylaw builder, and audit ledgers. Full visibility across all sectors. Executive access to cross-estate financials, police liaison desks, and system telemetry.
Zone Admin
Sector Secretariats		 Zone-level resident onboarding, street definitions, zonal levy billing, local announcements, violation citations, and local bank settlement tracking. Strict Zero-Leakage: Zero access to other zones' residents, ledgers, or streets. Query layer strictly bound by $_SESSION['zone_id'].

Staff & Gatehouse
Security Officers		 Sub-5-second QR code scanner, 6-digit OTP verification, vehicle license plate search, incident logging, and emergency dispatch acknowledgement. Operational gate terminal only. Strictly blocked from financial ledgers, private resident phone directories, and administrative settings.

Zero-Data & Power Failure Resiliency

Failover A • Cellular SMS Relay

SMS Passcode Redundancy

Visitor codes are delivered via standard cellular SMS to both guest and the gate backup GSM handset. If fiber internet drops, guards verify codes via estate GSM SMS line.

Failover B • Local Client Caching

IndexedDB & Offline Sync Queue

Gatehouse tablets cache active daily passes locally. Check-in timestamps store in local client memory and automatically sync to cloud ledgers once network reconnects.

Holographic RFID Resident Vehicle Stickers

Vehicle Security Feature Technical Implementation Operational Advantage
Serialized Holographic QR / Barcode Generated via car_sticker.php. Binds license plate, vehicle color, chassis number, and registered resident photo dossier. Prevents resident stickers from being swapped to unauthorized or commercial vehicles.
Instant Plate Search Engine Guard terminal features real-time plate lookup (api/search_vehicle.php) returning resident name and unit in < 200ms. Fast-tracks resident entry during peak rush hours (07:00–09:00 and 17:00–20:00).
1-Click Stolen Vehicle Blacklist Homeowners or security admins can instantly flag any car as "Compromised/Stolen" from the resident portal. Gate barrier locks immediately upon plate scan; audible alarm alerts armed guard patrol.

Security Guard Roster & Shift Operations

  • ✓ Post Assignments: Guards scheduled across Main Gate, Sector B Gatehouse, Patrol Team Alpha, and Visitor Inspection Bay.
  • ✓ Clock-In Telemetry: Guard arrival timestamps and biometric/device clock-in logs monitored live in admin/roster.php.
  • ✓ Live Emergency Snapshot: The active duty roster is automatically snapshotted whenever a resident panic alert is triggered.

4-Tier Target Stakeholder Routing

Stakeholder Scope Alarm & Dispatch Behavior Primary Use Cases
all_residents
Estate-Wide Alarm		 Full audible klaxon on guard terminals + high-priority WhatsApp broadcast + pinned marquee alert on all resident portal screens. Armed robbery attempt, major fire outbreak, gas explosion, or unauthorized armed intrusion.
guards_only
Silent Guard Alarm		 Silent alert sent directly to duty patrol officers and gatehouse terminals with GPS pin. Zero community siren triggered. Hostage situation, prowler / domestic burglary in progress where sirens could endanger victims.
guards_and_admin

guards_and_medical
Medical ER Cascade		 Dispatches gate patrol while auto-forwarding resident address & GPS coordinates to designated medical emergency hospital. Cardiac arrest, sudden health collapse, domestic accident requiring rapid ambulance escort.
